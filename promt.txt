## Product Vision

Shikho English is an AI-powered personal English teacher for Bangladeshi SSC students.

The initial MVP focuses on SSC English 2nd Paper grammar and question analysis.

The AI teacher should:
1. Analyze English questions.
2. Return the correct answer with a step-by-step explanation.
3. Explain grammar rules in simple Bangla and English.
4. Identify common mistakes.
5. Generate related practice questions.
6. Evaluate student answers and explain errors.
7. Save question history and track learning progress.

Future features may include English 1st Paper, writing evaluation, verified board questions, image/PDF question extraction, personalized revision, and student analytics.
